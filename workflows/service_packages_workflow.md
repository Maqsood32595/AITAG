## 1. Executive Summary & Market Positioning

AITAG bridges the gap between everyday Indian small businesses and advanced AI capabilities. Instead of forcing clients to learn prompt engineering or pay unpredictable hourly freelance rates, AITAG offers **productised, fixed-outcome AI service packages** priced fairly for the Indian market in Indian Rupees (**₹**).

### Core Brand Identity
* **Motto:** *"You do not need to learn AI. Tell us what your business needs, and AITAG will connect you with experts who can deliver it."*
* **Core Pillars:**
  * **SIMPLE:** Clear packages and everyday language (zero technical jargon).
  * **TRUSTED:** Vetted providers, human quality checks, and escrowed payments.
  * **PRACTICAL:** Ready-to-use business outcomes that increase revenue or save time.

## 4. Operating Safeguards & Marketplace Rules

1. **Strict Fixed Scope:** Every tier lists exact deliverables and revision caps. Custom extras require admin-issued change quotes.
2. **Customer Input Checklist:** The delivery turnaround clock starts **only after** the customer uploads all required input files (photos, logo, brief).
3. **Escrow Holding & Auto-Release:** Customer payment is held by AITAG. Funds are released upon customer approval, or auto-released after **5 days** if the customer is unresponsive and has not raised a dispute.
4. **Transparent Third-Party Expenses:** Domain, hosting, Meta WhatsApp API per-message charges, and tool subscription fees are strictly customer-paid and registered in the customer's own name for full ownership.
5. **AITAG Revenue Margin:** 5% commission deducted from the agreed price upon order payout.
