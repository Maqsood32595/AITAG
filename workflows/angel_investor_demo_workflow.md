## 1. Investor Proof Points & Validation Metrics

The AITAG MVP is instrumented around proof points designed to demonstrate commercial viability and capital efficiency:

| Metric | What it Proves to Investors | Target Pilot Benchmark |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Qualified Customer Requests** | Real demand for productised AI services among non-technical SMBs | 25+ requests in 14 days |
| **Request-to-Paid Conversion** | Willingness to pay without founder price discounting | > 40% conversion |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | Commercial expansion potential across Beginner, Medium, Expert | ₹2,500 – ₹6,000 |
| **Gross Margin %** | Marketplace unit economics (5% launch fee scaling to 15–20%) | Scalable take-rate model |
| **Time to Expert Match** | Operational responsiveness and supply-side liquidity | < 4 hours from request |
| **On-Time Delivery Rate** | Reliability of vetted freelancer network | > 90% delivered on schedule |
| **Revision Rate** | Clarity of fixed scope and customer alignment | < 1.2 revisions per order |
| **Customer CSAT / Testimonials** | Organic retention, repeat buying, and word-of-mouth referral | > 4.7 / 5.0 rating |

## 2. The 10-Step "Golden Path" Live Demo Script

When demonstrating AITAG to angel investors, do not click around aimlessly. Walk through this exact 10-step story:

### Step 1: Problem & Target Customer (The Hook)
* **Action:** Open Homepage.
* **Pitch:** *"India has 63 million small businesses. They know AI exists, but they don't have time to learn prompts or hire expensive full-time agencies. AITAG delivers ready business outcomes starting at ₹499."*

### Step 2: The Productised Catalogue
* **Action:** Scroll to the 3 launch packages.
* **Pitch:** *"Unlike Upwork or Fiverr where clients suffer through open bidding, AITAG packages outcomes with fixed scopes, delivery days, and transparent INR pricing."*

### Step 3: Package Deep Dive & Tier Selection
* **Action:** Click **"AI Product Photo Makeover"**. Show Beginner (₹499), Medium (₹1,499), and Expert (₹3,999) tabs.
* **Pitch:** *"The client sees exactly what is included, what is excluded, and how many revisions they get."*

### Step 4: The Frictionless Brief Submission
* **Action:** Click "Request Package". Fill brief for *"Organic Honey Co."*, select Beginner tier, upload a raw jar photo.
* **Pitch:** *"A 60-second brief submission captures all assets upfront so the turnaround clock starts with zero delays."*

### Step 5: Operations Behind the Scenes (The Admin Tracker)
* **Action:** Switch to `/admin/tracker`.
* **Pitch:** *"Behind the scenes, AITAG operates a lean control center. Founders match requests to pre-vetted specialists in seconds without expensive automation."*

### Step 6: Expert Assignment & Scope Confirmation
* **Action:** Click "Assign Specialist" $\rightarrow$ order status moves to `MATCHED`.

### Step 7: Payment Collection & Activation
* **Action:** Toggle payment status $\rightarrow$ order status becomes `ACTIVE`.
* **Pitch:** *"Funds are safely escrowed. The expert is protected, and the client knows we only release payout on approval."*

### Step 8: Workspace Delivery & Collaboration
* **Action:** Switch to Project Workspace. Show expert submitting studio cutout drafts with white background.

### Step 9: Customer Acceptance & Ratings
* **Action:** Click "Approve & Download Final Assets". Enter 5-star rating and testimonial.
* **Pitch:** *"Once approved, final high-resolution files unlock instantly."*

### Step 10: Unit Economics & Metrics Dashboard
* **Action:** Open Metrics View.
* **Pitch:** *"On this ₹499 transaction, AITAG recorded its platform fee and expert payout. This repeatable, operations-assisted flywheel proves demand and margin before heavy engineering."*
