## 1. Executive Summary & Market Mechanics

### The Core Constraints
1. **High AI Leverage (85%–95% Automated):** Every service utilizes automated AI pipelines (LLMs, generative diffusion, voice synthesis, vectorization) reducing human production time to 5–15 minutes per order.
2. **Strictly Time-Bound Delivery:** All deliverables completed within **2 to 6 days** (Starter: 2–4 days, Pro: 4–6 days).
3. **Zero Post-Delivery Responsibility:** Clean hand-off of source code, image archives, vector files, or video MP4s. No servers to maintain, no databases to patch, no community management, no monthly retainer headaches.
4. **Binary Pricing (Only 2 Prices per Service):** 
   - **Starter Tier (₹1,499 / ₹1,999):** Low-friction impulse purchase; builds trust for Indian SMBs.
   - **Pro Tier (₹3,499 / ₹4,999):** High-value comprehensive package; drives 60%+ of revenue.
   - Eliminates WhatsApp decision paralysis and enables instantaneous UPI payments (GPay/PhonePe/Paytm).

## 4. Operational Guardrails & Delivery Rules

1. **Strict Deliverable Hand-off:** Every order concludes with the delivery of final source files (ZIP archive, PDF, SVG, MP4) or ownership transfer (Cloudflare Pages/Netlify). No ongoing client hosting or management accounts are retained.
2. **Clock Starts on Assets Received:** Turnaround timers (24h/48h) initiate strictly after the customer uploads their questionnaire, raw photos, and business details.
3. **Third-Party Disclaimers:** Domain registration costs (e.g. ₹800/yr on GoDaddy/Hostinger) and physical card printing expenses are paid directly by the client in their own name.
4. **Revision Boundaries:** Each tier includes fixed revisions (1 for Starter, 2 for Pro) covering text/color adjustments. Fundamental redesigns or scope additions require a separate new order.
